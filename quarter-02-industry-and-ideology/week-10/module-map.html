📖 MODULE OVERVIEW
What happens when a nation learns to produce more than it ever has before?
Welcome to Quarter 2. This quarter turns from political revolution to a different, quieter kind of upheaval: a transformation in how people farmed, made things, and organized their labor, one that reshaped Europe as thoroughly as any war or declaration. Week 10 asks how a revolution in farming, driven by new methods and new field arrangements, fed a growing population and freed hands for other work. Week 11 asks what happened once machines, not muscle, began doing that other work.
⚡ QUICK CHECK-IN
This quarter's virtue thread is responsibility: the challenge of stewarding new power, wealth, and productive capacity well rather than carelessly. Before you know any specifics, what do you imagine could go wrong when a society suddenly produces far more than it used to? (No wrong answer.)
