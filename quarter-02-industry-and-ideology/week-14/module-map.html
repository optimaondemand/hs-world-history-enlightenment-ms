📖 MODULE OVERVIEW
Is a nation made by shared ideals, or by conquest and iron?
By the middle of the nineteenth century, two peninsulas of Europe that had been fractured for centuries into small states, kingdoms, and city-states each became a single nation within a decade of each other. This module tells both stories side by side. Week 14 asks how, comparing Italy's unification, driven substantially by nationalist ideals and popular uprising, against Germany's unification, driven substantially by Prussian statecraft, war, and the deliberate policy Otto von Bismarck called iron and blood. Week 15 then turns from nation-building at home to empire-building abroad, asking what Europe's newly confident nations told themselves they were doing when they extended that same power over other peoples.
