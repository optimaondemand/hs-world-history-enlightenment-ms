📖 MODULE OVERVIEW
Does a revolution's founding document settle its future, or only open the argument?
Module 3 ended with the Third Estate declaring itself the National Assembly. This module watches what that Assembly actually wrote, and how quickly the revolution it launched began to outrun its own founding text. Week 7 puts the Declaration of the Rights of Man side by side with the American Declaration you already know well, asking what each document borrowed and what each left out. Week 8 turns to Napoleon Bonaparte, the man who would claim to complete the Revolution's work while also, in the eyes of many contemporaries, ending it.
⚡ QUICK CHECK-IN
A nation writes down a list of rights it promises to respect. Does writing the list down make those rights secure, or is the list only the beginning of a much longer argument about what the words actually mean in practice? (No wrong answer.)
