📖 MODULE OVERVIEW
How do we come to know what is true, and who gets to decide?
This module opens the course, and the Scientific Revolution, in two moves. Week 1 asks what actually convinced Europe to abandon a thousand years of Earth-centered astronomy: not a single proof, but a slow accumulation of observation, argument, and courage. Week 2 asks a harder question underneath that one: once you stop trusting inherited authority, how do you decide what to trust instead? Bacon and Descartes gave two different answers, and both still shape how you were taught to think.
⚡ QUICK CHECK-IN
This module's virtue thread is self-government: governing your own mind by evidence and reason rather than by habit or authority. How comfortable are you, honestly, with changing your mind once you have already settled on an answer? (No wrong answer, just check in with yourself before you start.)
