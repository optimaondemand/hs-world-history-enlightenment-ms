1.5
Galileo, Dialogue Concerning the Two Chief World Systems (1632)
Primary Source
📖 BEFORE YOU READ

In Lesson 1.3 you read about Galileo's telescope and his trial. This source shows his other argument, the one made with pure reasoning rather than observation. If the Earth truly spins and hurtles around the sun, why does nothing about daily life feel like motion at all? Galileo answers through his character Salviati, who speaks for the Copernican position, in conversation with Sagredo, an open-minded listener.

As you read, look for the exact reason Galileo gives for why a shared, steady motion cannot be felt from inside it.

💡 ABOUT THIS TRANSLATION
Galileo wrote in Italian. The text below comes from the first English translation, made in 1661 by Thomas Salusbury, less than twenty years after Galileo's death. English spelling was not yet standardized in 1661, so you will see words spelled differently than you would today: onely for only, peice for piece, moveable for movable, Cœlestial for celestial. Read it slowly and sound out unfamiliar spellings before assuming a word is unfamiliar.
📚 FROM THE SECOND DAY OF THE DIALOGUE

“Whatsoever motion may be ascribed to the Earth, it is necessary that it be to us, (as inhabitants upon it, and consequently partakers of the same,) altogether imperceptible, and as if it were not at all, so long as we have regard onely to terrestrial things.”

Salviati, Dialogue Concerning the Two Chief World Systems, trans. Thomas Salusbury (1661), p. 97

“Motion is so far Motion, and as Motion operateth, by how far it hath relation to things which want Motion: but in those things which all equally partake thereof it hath nothing to do, and is as if it never were. And thus the Merchandises with which a ship is laden, so far move, by how far leaving London, they pass by France, Spain, Italy, and sail to Aleppo, which London, France, Spain &c. stand still, not moving with the ship: but as to the Chests, Bales and other Parcels, wherewith the ship is stow'd and laden, and in respect of the ship it self, the Motion from London to Syria is as much as nothing; and nothing altereth the relation which is between them: and this, because it is common to all, and is participated by all alike.”

Salviati, p. 98

“Who would believe, that Nature (which by common consent, doth not that by many things, which may be done by few) hath chosen to make an innumerable number of most vast bodies move, and that with an unconceivable velocity, to perform that, which might be done by the moderate motion of one alone about its own Centre?”

Salviati, pp. 99–100

“The same you see to ensue, in making the experiment in a ship with a bullet shot upwards perpendicularly with a Crosse-bow, which returneth to the same place whether the ship doth move, or stand still.”

Salviati, pp. 153–154
💡 WORDS YOU MIGHT NOT KNOW
onely: only.
peice: piece, here meaning a cannon.
quiescence: a state of rest.
Cœlestial: celestial, having to do with the heavens.
Terrestrial Globe: the Earth.
want Motion: lack motion, are at rest.
