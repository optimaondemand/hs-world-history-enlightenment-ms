📖 MODULE OVERVIEW
Did Napoleon spread the Revolution, or just its army?
This short, single-week module closes Quarter 1 by asking what Napoleon's conquests actually exported. Wherever his armies marched, from Italy to the Rhineland to the Netherlands, French administrators brought the Civil Code with them, along with legal equality and an end to feudal privilege. They also brought foreign occupation, heavy taxation, and conscription into French wars. This module asks you to weigh those two exports against each other, using both the Code's own reach and a fierce, defiant German response to it.
